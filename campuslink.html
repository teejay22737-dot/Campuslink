How it works

Three steps from a problem to a solved problem.

  1. Post or browse

    Offer a skill you have, or describe what you need. Browse the board by category.

  2. Connect

    Tap Contact on a card to see how to reach the student. Agree on scope and price up front.

  3. Get it done

    Meet on campus, deliver the work, and pay as agreed. Then help the next person.

Stay safe

  • Meet in public campus spots like the library, cafeteria or department lobby.
  • Agree on price and deliverables in writing before work starts.
  • For large jobs, pay in stages instead of everything upfront.
  • Never share passwords, ID numbers or card details.
